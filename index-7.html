What I Fix

Full-service solar repair

01

System Diagnostics

I'll assess your entire system — panels, inverter, wiring, and monitoring — and give you a straight answer on what's wrong and what it'll take to fix it.

02

Inverter Repair & Replacement

The inverter is the most common failure point. Whether it needs a reset, a firmware update, or a full swap, I handle it — across all major brands.

03

Monitoring Reconnection

If your monitoring portal died with your installer, I'll get you back on a live dashboard so you can actually see what your system is doing.

04

Warranty & Manufacturer Claims

Equipment warranties survive even when installers don't. I'll navigate manufacturer claims on your behalf and get you what you're owed.

Panel Inspection & Repair
